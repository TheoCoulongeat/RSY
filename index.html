Who we are

RSY (Rodriguez Ship Yard) is a shipyard on the Mediterranean coast. We mould and finish composite hulls for leisure boating, from fishing dinghies to premium cruisers, and we sell them to boat dealers who bring them to sailors.

Our synopsis is simple: a solid French shipyard, a range named after the Three Musketeers, and a team of ten partners who run the company together. Every hull leaves our workshops with the same promise written on our badge: Beyond the Waves, a boat that takes you further, safely and in comfort.

“We act with autonomy every day in our own team, we cooperate across all teams, and we decide together, by vote, on everything that shapes our common future.”Sara Rodriguez, CEO of RSY

Our philosophy and values

Our model combines the strength of the group with operational efficiency. Each partner has a clear role and real freedom within it; the decisions that shape our future are voted by all ten.

Transparency

We share every useful piece of information, with our team and with our dealers.

Participation

Everyone takes part in discussions and collective votes. Major commitments need 8 votes out of 10.

Solidarity

When one team faces a peak of work, the others step in. All for one, one for all.

Trust and learning

Mistakes are never hidden or used to blame; we study them calmly to work better together.

Climate · Admitted November 2026

Convention des Entreprises pour le Climat

RSY has been admitted to the Provence Méditerranée programme of the Convention des Entreprises pour le Climat (CEC), following its application and selection interview. Over six collective sessions across eight months, our management and an internal partner will build a structured, measurable roadmap with other business leaders of the region.

  • Opening session: 3 December 2026
  • Focus: eco-design, cleaner retrofitting, responsible industrial practices
  • Already in place: industrial-waste sorting and workshop energy savings
  • Under study: rooftop solar panels, local plastic and timber sourcing, cycling and car-sharing incentives
Diversity · Partner member

Elles Bougent

RSY is a partner member of the Elles Bougent association, which encourages girls to choose engineering and technical careers. Our women engineers and technicians volunteer as mentors, and we host a Girls in Naval Engineering Day for 40 middle- and high-school students at our Toulon site.

  • Mentoring by RSY women engineers and technicians
  • At least two outreach actions each year
